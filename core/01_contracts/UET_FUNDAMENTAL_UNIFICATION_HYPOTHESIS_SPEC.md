# UET Fundamental-Unification Hypothesis Track

## Status

`HYPOTHESIS_TRACK / BLOCKED`

This track cannot promote or invalidate the primary covariant effective theory.
It asks whether the effective response scalar and lane-specific collective
coordinates can be embedded consistently in a larger local quantum field theory.

## Current field content

- Available internally: Lorentz metric input, candidate scalar response `Phi`,
  global O(2) scalar matter doublet, tree-level finite-density O(2) EOS, and
  standard photon source-propagation-detector control.
- Not available: Standard-Model gauge group representation, local gauge
  connection and field strength, Dirac/spinor parent action, chiral matter
  content, BRST/gauge fixing, anomaly cancellation, loop renormalization, or a
  source-detector derivation for UET-specific particles.

## Mandatory gates

1. Declare fields and representations under Lorentz and local gauge groups.
2. Derive one action with bounded Hamiltonian and no ghost/tachyon modes.
3. Derive conserved currents and Ward identities.
4. Check charge conjugation, parity, time reversal, and CPT conventions.
5. Check gauge and gravitational anomalies for the complete chiral spectrum.
6. State power counting, cutoff, counterterm basis, and renormalization status.
7. Derive mass eigenstates rather than naming `Phi`, `C`, or `R_gen` as particles.
8. Provide source-propagation-detector observables and falsification conditions.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- `R_gen` is not photon, neutrino, positron, antimatter, or a gauge field.
- Global O(2) symmetry is not evidence of a local Standard-Model gauge sector.
- A tree-level mean-field EOS is not a unitarity or renormalization proof.
- Passing a standard photon baseline is not a UET particle prediction.
- A scalar effective mass shift is not a derivation of fermion mass generation.

## Independence rule

Failure of this track leaves a separately closed effective theory intact. The
track may be promoted only when every relevant symmetry, unitarity, anomaly,
renormalization, and observable gate has its own machine-readable evidence.
