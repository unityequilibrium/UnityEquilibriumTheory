**Status:** ONTOLOGY_CONTRACT / PHYSICAL_CLOSURE_BLOCKED

This specification defines the smallest common contract for the primary
covariant effective-theory track. It does not claim a fundamental derivation,
empirical validation, or global-universe openness.

## Minimal postulates

### UET-P0 ? Declared physical state

A physical model declares its state variables, transformation laws, units,
domain, and interactions. Mathematical coordinates are not physical quantities
until a lane-specific correspondence is supplied.

Standard counterpart: state-space modeling and field representations.

Falsification: the declared state is insufficient to reproduce deterministic or
statistical future predictions under fixed inputs.

Not assumed: that every state coordinate is matter, energy, information, or an
observable.

### UET-P1 ? Lane-specific collective coordinate

The collective coordinate is produced by a declared coarse-graining or
measurement operator from a microscopic or lower-level state. The map may be
many-to-one.

Standard counterpart: order parameters, hydrodynamic densities, Noether-charge
densities, and effective collective variables.

Falsification: no stable/refinement-consistent map exists in the declared lane.

Not assumed: C is universal mass, energy, force, probability, or charge.

### UET-P2 ? Conservative generator

The conservative sector is generated by a declared action, Hamiltonian, or
variational functional. Equations presented as one conservative theory must be
derivatives of the same generator.

Standard counterpart: Lagrangian/Hamiltonian field theory and variational
continuum mechanics.

Falsification: field equations, currents, reciprocal sources, or stress-energy
cannot be recovered from the declared generator.

### UET-P3 ? Open-system reduction

Dissipation, noise, memory, and irreversible transport require an explicit
open-system reduction or constitutive record with provenance.

Standard counterpart: Schwinger?Keldysh/KMS effective theory, GKSL dynamics,
Mori?Zwanzig memory, and nonequilibrium thermodynamics.

Falsification: positivity, entropy, fluctuation?dissipation, or exchange-ledger
conditions fail.

Not assumed: a normalized damping term is microscopic dissipation.

### UET-P4 ? Causal physical influence

Physical influence propagates through a declared field, current, excitation, or
channel with causal support and conservation/exchange accounting.

Standard counterpart: relativistic causal propagation and source?field?detector
dynamics.

Falsification: influence precedes the declared cone or has no source/channel
ledger.

Not assumed: photon, neutrino, positron, or generated trace are identical.

### UET-P5 ? Detector-generated record

The observer record is generated by a detector/receiver interaction with a
carrier. Observer metadata alone does not alter the source event or generated
trace.

Standard counterpart: operational measurement theory and detector response.

Falsification: changing only the observer protocol changes source dynamics.

Not assumed: records are an independent cosmic substance.

### UET-P6 ? Operational quantum probability

Quantum predictions use a valid density operator, channel, instrument/POVM, and
Born probabilities before any interpretation is attached.

Standard counterpart: operational quantum mechanics.

Falsification: positivity, complete positivity, normalization, no-signalling,
or standard quantum benchmarks fail.

Not assumed: QBism or RQM changes the physical propagation equations.

### UET-P7 ? Derived persistence selection

Persistence is a lifetime/selection property derived from physical dynamics,
resource exchange, and failure/transition criteria. It is not intent.

Standard counterpart: stochastic thermodynamics, survival analysis, dynamical
selection, and nonequilibrium statistical mechanics.

Falsification: the declared physical resource/failure map does not predict
persistence beyond a baseline or holdout.

Not assumed: a universal least-energy or potential-energy preservation law.

### UET-P8 ? Competing closed/non-closed branches

The exact closed-response branch is H0; a non-zero response/exchange branch is
H1. The existence of H1 does not establish that nature selects it.

Standard counterpart: nested-model testing and covariant stress-energy balance.

Falsification: the closed limit is discontinuous, violates the parent equations,
or the alternative fails independent comparison after parameter penalties.

Not assumed: the complete universe has an exterior or is globally open.

## Interpretation boundary

Operational quantum mechanics is the prediction baseline. QBism and relational
quantum mechanics may be represented as interpretation records after the
preparation?channel?instrument?outcome contract is fixed. Interpretation
metadata must not change empirical probabilities unless a separate physical
model is explicitly introduced.

## Closure boundary

Passing the ontology gate means the postulates are complete, non-circular, and
mapped to standard theory. It does not mean their physical realization,
dimensional map, numerical well-posedness, or empirical support has passed.
