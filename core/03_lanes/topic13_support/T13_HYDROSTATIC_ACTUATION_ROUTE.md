## Measured direction versus full tensor

For the source axial-modulus convention the hydrostatic strain row is (-2/B_a,-1/B_c). Data fix its contraction with (D_ab,D_c). Null-direction changes to that pair cannot change the contraction, although they change response to other strains. Therefore missing full tensor components do not prevent use of this measured hydrostatic spectral projection. The separate missing O(2) mode identity remains essential.

The two mode derivatives are1.6806663e-52 and5.8242423e-51 J^2/Pa. Union sensitivity envelopes are[1.4592186e-52,1.8857777e-52] and[5.4795278e-51,6.2346281e-51]. These small SI numbers reflect squared quantum mode energies, not weak or well-measured UET coupling. Table/logarithmic and abstract/absolute-slope uncertainties are both retained; the envelope is not a confidence interval.
