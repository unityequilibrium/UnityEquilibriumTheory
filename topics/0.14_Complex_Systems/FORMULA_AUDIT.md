## Current Verifier Boundary

- Current artifact: `Result/artifacts/0_14_complex_systems_verification.json`.

## Unit and Data Discipline

- HRV RR intervals are treated as seconds after cleaning to `0.3 < rr < 2.0`.
- SOC grid state is dimensionless and step-based.
- Econophysics price dynamics are simulation units unless mapped to a declared market dataset and date range.
- Cross-domain scores are not dimensionally comparable without per-domain normalization and baseline definitions.
