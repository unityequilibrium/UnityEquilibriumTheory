## 🚥 3. Layered 3D Traffic Management

To prevent collisions in high-traffic zones (e.g., Earth-Heliopause), we implement **Vertical Separation**:

*   **Layer Z+1 (North Track):** Outbound traffic (towards gas giants/deep space).
*   **Layer Z-1 (South Track):** Inbound traffic (towards Earth/Sun).
*   **Layer Z=0 (Ecliptic):** Inter-planetary orbital parking/docks.

## 🔋 4. The "Free Energy" Harvesting Grid (Topic 0.37)

The UTN is powered by the **Solar Paint Infrastructure**:

1.  **Station Coating:** Every structural pylon and station at Lagrange points is coated in Topic 0.37 Solar Paint.
2.  **Radiation Trap:** The Sun wastes ~99% of its energy into empty space. Our UTN "Subway" acts as a large-scale collector, harvesting this "wasted" radiation.
3.  **Wireless Power (WET):** Energy is transmitted to ships via **Synchronized Acoustic Waves (SAW)** through the spacetime lattice itself.
