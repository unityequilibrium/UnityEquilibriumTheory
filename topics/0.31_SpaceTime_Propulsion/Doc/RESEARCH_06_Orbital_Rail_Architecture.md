## 1. 📄 Executive Summary (บทคัดย่อผู้บริหาร)

> **"Travel between worlds should be as seamless as a train ride across a city."**

*   **Problem:** Planetary orbits are dynamic and crowded. Traditional "line-of-sight" travel causes collision risks and orbital interference.
*   **Solution:** **"Orbital Hill Rails"**. Anchoring transport hubs at the stable "hill" points between gravity wells and connecting them with a geometric "Double-Circle & Square Port" rail system.
*   **Result:** A collision-free, high-speed overpass highway that connects all planets in a single synchronized loop.

## ⛰️ 2. The "Spacetime Hills": Between-Orbit Stability (เนินอวกาศ)

The user correctly identified that "between planets" there are points of stability (High ground).

*   **The Hill Logic:** In a gravity well (Planet), spacetime is skewed "downward." In the space between two planets, there is a relative "hill" or plateau where the net gravitational pull is balanced.
*   **Stationary Anchoring:** Using the **Lattice Locking (0.4)** mentioned previously, we place our Port Hubs on these hills. This ensures the Ports stay fixed relative to the solar system's center, even as the planets move around them.

## 🛤️ 3. Architecture: The "Double-Circle & Square Port" (รางคู่และการเชื่อมต่อ)

The user proposed a specific geometry: Two concentric circles linked by 4-sided rectangular (Square) ports.

*   **The Geometric Bridge:** 
    *   **Inner Circle:** A high-speed rail running "above" the inner planets (Mercury/Venus/Earth).
    *   **Outer Circle:** A rail for the outer planets (Mars/Jupiter/Saturn).
    *   **The Square Ports:** These are the massive Interchanges that bridge the gap between two orbits. They act as "elevators" or "shuttles" that transport containers from the faster outer ring to the inner ring.
*   **The "Double-Track" Effect:** Just like a standard train track with a platform in the middle, the Square Port allows vehicles from the Inner and Outer rails to dock simultaneously, enabling rapid freight transfer.

## 🚄 4. High-Speed Overpasses (การวิ่งเร็วเหนือดาว)

*   **Vertical Segregation:** The rail system sits at a different "height" in the 4th dimension (Information Gradient) compared to the planets.
*   **Non-Interference:** This allows a Space Train to travel at relativistic speeds directly "over" Jupiter without its gravity well slowing the train down or the train's wake destabilizing Jupiter's moons.
