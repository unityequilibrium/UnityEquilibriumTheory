# Limitations — Topic 0.25

## Current controlling limitations

1. The systematic literature review and specialist historical appraisal are incomplete.
2. Legacy R/N/K/I proxies overlap conceptually and do not provide three independent measurement families.
3. The frozen WDI package has zero complete rows across the four required global indicators; global replication is blocked.
4. Revised historical macro series are not a real-time vintage panel, so predictive interpretation remains limited.
5. M2, credit, output, and inflation are endogenous; aggregate association does not identify fiat or policy causality.
6. Public payer, firm, award, shipment, and physical-resource sources do not form one tagged transaction-to-resource ledger.
7. Money is fungible; profit, debt, equity, or taxes cannot be assigned to a specific invoice without segregation or an explicit inference rule.
8. KLEMS/I-O/USEEIO provide sector accounting or modelled average footprints, not observed marginal causal paths.
9. CPI/PCE are defined basket indexes and do not represent every household's purchasing power.
10. EPI and BLS wage/productivity series have different universes, deflators, and constructions.
11. Energy throughput is not literal fuel density, useful energy, EROI, or exergy.
12. Licensed total-return asset inputs and redistribution rights remain unresolved.
13. Historical claims about firewood, mathematics, zero, intellectual transmission, and national motives remain quarantined.
14. Thailand labor-policy and restricted microdata lanes require privacy, legal, worker-rights, and ethics review.
15. No independent code-path replication or human publication sign-off exists.

## Interpretation ceiling

The package can report source-locked internal descriptive diagnostics and predictive failures under declared baselines. It cannot confirm an economic law, identify a universal value of money, establish fiat causality, trace untagged dollars, validate a policy, or validate a monetary peg.
