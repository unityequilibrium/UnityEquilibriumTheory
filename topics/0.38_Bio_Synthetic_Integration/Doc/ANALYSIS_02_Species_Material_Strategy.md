## 🏗️ 2. The Three-Tiered Hierarchy

### Tier 1: The Soul (Vitrification Layer)
*   **Model:** *Tardigrada* (หมีน้ำ)
*   **Material:** **TDPs (Tardigrade Disordered Proteins)**.
*   **UET Logic:** Using vitrification to "freeze" information states. 
*   **Application:** Protecting Bio-Synthetic processors (Topic 0.24) from extreme solar flares, vacuum desiccation, and thermal instability. It ensures the vehicle's "Instruction Set" remains coherent in deep space.

### Tier 2: The Shield (Exoskeleton/Armor)
*   **Model:** *Crustacea* (Shell/กระดอง)
*   **Material:** **Chitin-Graphene Mineral Composite**.
*   **UET Logic:** High-directional stiffness and energy absorption.
*   **Application:** Outer hulls for transmedium vehicles. Ideal for high-pressure deep sea (Topic 0.29) and aerospace reentry shields. The shell "flexes" to absorb impact, dissipating $dS$ before it reaches the core.

### Tier 3: The Spine (Endoskeleton/Support)
*   **Model:** *Vertebrata* (Bone)
*   **Material:** **Carbonated Hydroxyapatite (HAp)**.
*   **UET Logic:** High compressive strength and mineralization.
*   **Application:** The internal core of massive orbital docks (Topic 0.36) and large-scale habitation rings. It handles the primary load-bearing duties.
