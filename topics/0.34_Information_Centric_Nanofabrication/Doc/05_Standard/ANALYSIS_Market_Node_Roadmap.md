## 4. Why 28nm-45nm for Space?

The space industry (Satellites) actually prefers these nodes over <10nm because:
1.  **Radiation Hardness:** Larger transistors are physically more resistant to cosmic ray bit-flips.
2.  **Heat Dissipation:** Perovskite soft-lattices handle thermal cycles better at these scales.
3.  **Cost of Loss:** If a $50 satellite chip fails, you launch another. If a $5,000 3nm chip fails, the mission ROI crashes.
