# Limitations: 0.22 Biophysics & Origin of Life

- The primary verifier is a seeded synthetic biomarker diagnostic with a class-C internal benchmark ceiling.
- The current artifact does not use raw EEG, real omics, clinical endpoints, or prebiotic chemistry measurements.
- CHB-MIT, Bonn, and TCGA source records are provenance context; raw files, exact windows/cohorts, preprocessing, licenses, and hashes remain open.
- The local Bonn Z.txt and S.txt files are synthetic mechanics-test placeholders, not authenticated upstream dataset files.
- Normalized entropy, coherence, stability, and complexity values do not automatically carry SI units or biological meaning.
- Homeostasis claims depend on an environment entropy ledger and the open dependency boundary to Topic 0.13.
- Origin-of-life scripts impose patterns or use proxy fields; they do not demonstrate emergent chemistry.
- TCGA and clinical extensions use mock or virtual data and are quarantined from current evidence.
- The HP lane now has an exact optimum and deterministic internal artifact for one topic-local sequence, but it still lacks real protein structure data, physical free-energy correspondence, PDB/CASP benchmarking, and independent replication.
- The protein-folding dynamics lane is currently a class-B source/runtime preflight. No source-locked 12-protein cohort, atomistic runtime, force-field asset hash, or smoke-test result is present.
- The proposed UET dynamics terms T22-013 through T22-018 are candidate lane mappings. They are not atomistic laws, SI free energy, microscopic cellular transport derivations, or biological validation.
- Internal execution does not establish external replication, formal proof, clinical utility, or broad physical closure.
