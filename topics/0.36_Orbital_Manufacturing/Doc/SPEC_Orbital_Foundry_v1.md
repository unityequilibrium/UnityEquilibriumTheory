# 🛰️ SPEC: UET Orbital Foundry v1.0 (Solar Base Architecture)

> **"Infrastructure as an Organism: Self-Replicating, Self-Cooling, and Self-Sustaining."**

## 1. 🏗️ Station Configuration: The "Axiom Gateway"
The Orbital Foundry is a modular, high-bandwidth fabrication hub designed for zero-G atomic assembly.

### 📐 Physical Specifications
- **Core Module**: i7-Hyperion 1-Million Nozzle Array (X-Y-Z stage optimized for vacuum).
- **Cooling System**: Passive Radiative Shields (3K base) + Active Dilution Refrigeration (10mK target via He-3).
- **Energy Input**: **Topic 0.37 Solar Paint (Perovskite-Graphene)** coated hull.
- **Atmosphere**: Ultra-High Vacuum (UHV) as native environment.

## 🌡️ 2. Twin-Zone Industrial Topology
The Foundry is split into two specialized thermal zones linked via Topic 0.31 logistics.

### 🔥 Hot Zone (Solar Harvest Hub)
- **Location**: Proximity to Sun (Low Solar Orbit).
- **Primary Function**: MW-scale Energy Harvesting & Bulk Smelting.
- **Component**: Solar Paint Coated 'Power Collector' stations.
- **Thermal Logic**: Graphene-Plasma shields + High-Efficiency Radiators.

### ❄️ Cold Zone (Atomic Fabrication Hub)
- **Location**: Solar Shadow / Deep Space (L1-L2 Lagrange Points).
- **Primary Function**: 10.8pm Precision Printing (Topic 0.34).
- **Thermal Logic**: 10mK Dilution Refrigerator powered by 'Energy Canisters' shipped from the Hot Zone.

### 🔌 Industrial Stingray (Collector Units)
- **Role**: Sweeping LEO debris and lunar silicates.
- **Painting**: Automated spray-heads for applying **Topic 0.37 Solar Paint** to new hull modules.
- **Output**: RAW carbon strings and purified Silicon for the 1M nozzle head.

### 🔄 Recursive Maintenance (Seed Factory)
- The station can print its own **Replacement IDT (Interdigital Transducer)** masks using the SAW phase-locking engine.
- If a nozzle fails, the system auto-reroutes firing logic (Axiom 5) and prints a repair patch.

## 🏗️ 3. The "Lunar Loop" (He-3 Supply Chain)
To maintain the **10.8pm precision**, the station requires constant Helium-3.

- **Extraction**: Semi-autonomous "Lunar Rovers" sweep the Regolith.
- **Refinement**: Solar-thermal distillation of He-3.
- **Transport**: **Orbital Rail Slingshot (0.31)** delivers He-3 canisters to the Foundry every 14 days.

## 📝 4. Operational Metrics
- **Target Precision**: 10.8 picometers (Atomic limit).
- **Target Throughput**: 3,500 WPH (Wafers Per Hour).
- **Autonomy Level**: **Level 5 (Full Solar Autonomy)** - No human presence required.
