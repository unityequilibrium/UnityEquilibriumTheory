# SPEC: Terminal Pluto - The High-Arch Zenith Hub (Topic 0.36)

## 1. 🏗️ Structural Foundation: Lattice-Locked Pylons (LLP)

Because Pluto's surface is volatile (Nitrogen/Methane icing), traditional building foundations are insufficient.
*   **Axiom 10 Integration**: We use **Axiom 10 SpaceTime Pylons** to anchor the station not to the ice, but to the local gravitational well of the planet.
*   **The 'Pylon' Structure**: A vertical lattice structure that extends 2km above the surface, providing a low-G dock for Interstellar Ships ($~0.06g$).

---

## 🚀 2. Assembly Hangar: The "Zenith Shipyard"

Designed specifically for **Interstellar-Class TMVs** (Transit Managed Vehicles).
*   **Vacuum Assembly**: Components are 3D-manufactured at the base (using ICN Topic 0.34) and assembled at the Pylon docks.
*   **Interstellar Hull Prep**: Application of **Bio-Skins (Topic 0.30)** and **Solar Paint (Topic 0.37)** as the final layer.
*   **Magnetic Rail (High-Gravity Exit)**: A 500m magnetic rail that provides the initial "nudge" out of the Pluto well (1.2 km/s exit velocity required).

---

## 🔋 3. Energy & Fuel: The "Deep Cold Storage"

*   **He-3 Fuel Line**: The station is fed by a continuous logistics line from **Neptune** (Topic 0.31), storing liquid Helium-3 in subterranean vacuum-insulated cryo-tanks.
*   **Artificial Sun Core (Topic 0.32)**: A small-scale fusion core powers the entire shipyard, providing continuous heat and electrical power in the dark Kuiper Belt.
*   **UET Quantum Bridge**: High-bandwidth data link between Pluto and Earth for real-time AI Traffic Control (Topic 0.35).
