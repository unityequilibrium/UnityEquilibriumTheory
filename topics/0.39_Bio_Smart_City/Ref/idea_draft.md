# Raw Materials & Initial Ideas: Water City

## Core Concepts (By USER)
- **Model:** Water City (เมืองน้ำ)
- **Primary Goals:**
    1. **Flood Mitigation:** Solve flooding by creating habitats that rise with or utilize water.
    2. **Land De-density:** Reduce land overcrowding by utilizing water surfaces for urban expansion.
    3. **Green Space Expansion:** Use biological integration to increase greenery in areas that are currently purely industrial or concrete-dense.

## UET Integration Points
- **Topic 0.29 (Ocean Recovery):** Desalination and water purification integrated into the city's base.
- **Topic 0.30 (Mega Flora):** Vertical gardens and "Living Walls" that produce oxygen and manage microclimates.
- **Topic 0.31 (Propulsion/SpaceTime):** Possible use of advanced fluid dynamics for wave mitigation.
- **Topic 0.32 (Micro Fusion):** Powering the city independently from the mainland grid.
- **Topic 0.38 (Bio-Synthetic Integration):** Using living structural materials (Bone-chassis/Hydroxyapatite) for floating platforms that are self-healing and carbon-negative.
