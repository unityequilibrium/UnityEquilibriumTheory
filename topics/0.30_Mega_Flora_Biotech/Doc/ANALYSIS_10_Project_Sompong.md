## 🌴 2. Bio-Geotechnical Profile: Sompong

### 2.1 Soft-Wood Flexiblity (ศักยภาพไม้เนื้ออ่อน)
Unlike hard-metals or hard-woods, the Sompong's soft-wood structure allows it to **dissipate energy** from moving water.
- **Damping Factor:** The flexible fibers act as a natural shock absorber for water surges during floods.
- **Canal Stabilization:** The roots "weave" into the mud, creating a biological mesh that prevents soil from being washed away.

### 2.2 The Buttress Anchor (รากพูพอน ยึดตลิ่ง)
The 5-meter buttress roots are the ultimate "Soil Piles."
- **Lateral Stability:** By spreading surface area, they prevent the soil from shifting laterally (a major cause of canal collapse).
- **Urban Integration:** Ideal for "Canal-Cities" where foundations are threatened by rising water levels.
