# Method

## Problem target

This topic studies whether UET can act as a coherent cross-domain synthesis across the repository's foundational theory claims.

The current method is an integration dashboard. It runs selected subordinate engines and records their current metrics. It does not independently re-derive or re-verify the subordinate topics.

## Variable framing

- Primary modeled quantities: omega-like state variables, kappa-like coupling terms, entropy-style quantities, and cross-scale linking terms
- Current verifier-backed quantities: delegated component metrics such as Weinberg angle, tau mass, Bell-state entropy, Reynolds number, and atomic H-alpha error.

## Assumptions

- The topic relies on symbolic reasoning and cross-topic consistency rather than a single empirical benchmark pipeline.
- Subordinate topic metrics retain their own proof status, data status, and limitations.

## Domain of validity

- Internal synthesis, symbolic consistency checks, and integration of claims from subordinate topics.
- Current pass/fail interpretation is limited to the selected component-engine run contract.

## Excluded cases

- A full empirical closure across all downstream topics or a final unified physical theory.
- Any claim that all constants are derived, all scales are closed, or subordinate failures are resolved by integration.

## Parameter sensitivity note

- This topic inherits assumptions and calibration choices from subordinate topics and should not be described as parameter-free.
- The integration artifact must eventually read subordinate artifact statuses before being treated as a reliable dependency gate.
